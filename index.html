Specialist agents, one accountable system

Each agent has a job. None gets unlimited authority.

They share context through the campaign record, with transparent outputs and explicit review gates.

INTELLIGENCE

Research Desk

Maps audience tensions, category movement, competitor patterns, and the evidence needed to support a decision.

STRATEGY

Campaign Strategist

Turns signals into priorities, a focused hypothesis, success measures, and a route worth testing.

CREATIVE

Creative Flywheel

Builds approved campaign territories into content systems, channel variants, and reusable production briefs.

OPERATIONS

Launch Coordinator

Sequences approved work across the content calendar, publishing queue, promotion plan, and response paths.

ANALYTICS

Learning Engine

Compares outcomes to the hypothesis, identifies what changed, and proposes the next experiment.

GOVERNANCE

Approval Gate

Stops work requiring human judgment: public claims, spend changes, sensitive responses, or off-brand actions.

Autonomous where it is safe. AgencyOS can independently research, organize, draft, monitor, and recommend. Publishing, ad spend, legal or regulated claims, and sensitive customer actions remain human-approved until a business deliberately changes its rules.
