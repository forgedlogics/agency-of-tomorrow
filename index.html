CAMPAIGN STRATEGY

Objects with a past.

Business goal: Turn material curiosity into qualified waitlist demand before a recycled-silver collection launch.

Audience tension: The customer wants a piece with meaning, but “sustainable” language often feels vague or overly worthy.

Creative territory: Show the material as an object in transformation—silver with a visible former life, reshaped into an heirloom for the next one.

EXPERIMENT PLAN

Make the first move measurable.

Variable: The opening message.

A — Process proof: “This silver has already lived once.”

B — Exclusivity: “First access to a new kind of heirloom.”

Hold constant: Audience, landing page, visual, spend cap, and campaign period.

Decision rule: Keep the message that earns more qualified sign-ups after a meaningful sample; do not choose a winner from early clicks.
