THE TOOLS

From strategy to usable output.

Email

Subject lines, preheaders, sender details, images, alt text, CTA and footer checks. Preview and export HTML. Plan welcome, launch, post-purchase and reactivation flows.

Instagram + Facebook

Write platform-specific captions. Create portrait, square or landscape artwork using your own image or typography. Export PNGs and caption files.

Website + SEO

Shape search intent, page titles, descriptions, headings and helpful page copy. Build tracking links and export a landing-page draft.

Strategy + measurement

Choose a niche, build a customer-first brief and assess campaign economics. Record delivery, click, conversion, revenue and opt-out data manually.

Your work stays with you

Drafts are saved on this browser, not in a cloud account. Export a workspace backup before changing devices or clearing browser data. Avoid storing sensitive customer lists.

Know what is connected

AgencyOS does not automatically send email, publish posts, run ads, connect a store, crawl websites or pull live analytics. Exported content needs testing and setup in the destination platform.
