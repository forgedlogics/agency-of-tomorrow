01 STRATEGIC FOUNDATION

Make the material the main character.

Tide is not selling “eco jewelry.” It is offering an object with a visible past and a future worth keeping. The campaign makes that transformation tactile, personal, and collectible.

BUSINESS OBJECTIVEBuild a qualified waitlist before launch.
AUDIENCE TENSIONMeaningful jewelry is desired; worthy messaging is not.
CAMPAIGN HYPOTHESISProcess proof will outperform exclusivity language.

02 / THE CREATIVE TERRITORY

Objects with a past.

RAW / REFINED / REMEMBERED — Each piece carries the tension between the raw material it used to be and the heirloom it is becoming.

03 ART DIRECTION

A visual world for people who notice the details.

MATERIAL STUDY

Silver, up close.

Photograph the surface like a landscape: tool marks, softened edges, a light-catching imperfection. No generic product cutouts.

MOVEMENT

Water meets metal.

Use rippled reflections, hard flash, and cropped gestures. The motion should feel like a tide pulling something familiar into a new form.

CASTING

Hands, not models.

Show hands in the making and the wearing. Keep styling spare enough that the piece remains the emotional object.

TYPE SYSTEM

Quietly declarative.

Short statements, generous space, one precise fact at a time. The tone is assured, not instructional.
